# ADR-0000: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN, linked to its file
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The situation that forces a decision. State the constraint, the measurement or
the requirement that makes the question live. A reader who knows nothing about
the project should understand from this section why the decision could not be
avoided.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, in the present tense.

## Alternatives considered

Each credible alternative, with the reason it was not taken. An ADR is worth
writing precisely because a reviewer might reasonably have expected one of
these instead, so this section carries most of the value.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision, including what becomes harder. An ADR that
lists only benefits is a sales pitch rather than a record.
